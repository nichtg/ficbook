How It Works

1

Add Your Favourite Tags

Search for your favourite AO3 tags in Ficbook and add them to your watchlist. You can track as many tags as you want.

2

Choose Stories to Track

Browse stories in your tags or search for specific titles. Add your favourites to your personal library for detailed tracking.

3

Get Notified of Updates

Ficbook monitors AO3's RSS feeds and alerts you whenever new stories or chapters appear in your tracked content.

4

Enjoy Without Leaving

Click through directly to AO3 to read your favourite stories. All your tracking stays organized in Ficbook.
